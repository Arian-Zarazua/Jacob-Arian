# Basic NFL Data Analysis Workflow

## Step 1: identify football context
Determine whether question is about teams, players, games, or seasons.

## Step 2: map to dataset
Stats, Snapcounts, Scoring, ExpectedPoints, Seasons, Roster.

## Step 3: define unit of analysis
player, game, team, or season level.

## Step 4: clean identifiers
season, event_date, team alias, player IDs.

## Step 5: compute summaries
production, efficiency, turnovers, usage.

## Step 6: compare groups
team vs team, player vs player, or baseline.

## Step 7: interpret football meaning
context over raw numbers.

## Step 8: validate sanity
check bounds, duplicates, and grouping correctness.
